# 🎓 Teaching AI at Oak Ridge Missionary Baptist Church 🎓

This Saturday, July 25, I'm leading a beginner-friendly AI workshop for the congregation at **Oak Ridge Missionary Baptist Church** in Kansas City, KS — open to a room of 75-100 people spanning every age and comfort level with technology.

The theme: **Ask It. Use It. Guard It.**

## 📖 What We're Covering

- 🔥 Where AI actually came from, in plain English — no jargon
- 🔥 What tools like ChatGPT and Gemini really are, and how to talk to them
- 🔥 Practical everyday uses — decoding confusing mail, explaining health terms, saving time
- 🔥 The good, the bad, and the ugly — including how scammers now use AI to clone voices
- 🔥 A hands-on segment where attendees try it themselves, live, on their own phones

## ✅ Who It's For

Church members of every age and background — this isn't a tech conference. It's built for people who've never opened ChatGPT and want to walk in curious and walk out capable.

This is exactly why I do this work through **Apex Resolution**: practical AI education for everyday people and small businesses, not just the already-tech-fluent.

# 🚨 AI Scholarship Alert for Small Business Professionals 🚨

This is a major opportunity for professionals looking to level up their AI skills, certifications, and career growth in AI, workforce development, business, data analytics, prompt engineering, and more.

The **American Express Foundation** and **Scholarship America** have launched the **Smart Futures for Small Business Scholarship** to help small business employees access AI education and certification programs. Eligible participants may receive **$250–$1,000** toward tuition, books, and educational expenses.

## ✅ Who's Eligible?

- U.S. residents age 18+
- Employees of U.S.-based small businesses (500 employees or less)
- Planning to enroll in an AI-related certification or educational program by **September 1, 2026**

## 📚 Example Programs

- Generative AI
- AI for Business
- Prompt Engineering
- Data Analytics
- AI Marketing
- Digital Customer Success
- AI Generalist Programs
