# Apex Brand Voice Interview

**What this is:** The calibration instrument that produces the voice half of the Apex Signal Document. Modeled on the brand-voice-interview step of the Magnetic Demand Method (the first input into every signal document), extended with sections her version doesn't have: claim verification and technical-credibility calibration.

**How to run it:** Answer out loud in a recorded session (Fireflies on), not in writing. Spoken answers carry the phrasing, rhythm, and side-stories that make a voice document real. One sitting, 60–90 minutes. Don't polish — the throwaway lines are usually the voice.

**Rules for whoever processes the answers:**
- Capture exact phrases, not summaries. The output should quote Carlos, not describe him.
- Contradictions between answers are signal, not noise — flag them.
- Every claim that could appear in copy gets checked against `brand/_default/verified-claims.md` before it enters the voice document.

---

## Section 1 — Origin and conviction
*Feeds: voice document core, About section, talk-from-stage material.*

1. Tell the story of the moment you decided to start Apex Resolution. Where were you, what happened, what did you say to yourself?
2. What did 20 years inside Federal Reserve Bank and Sprint IT teach you that a small-business consultant who came up through marketing will never understand?
3. What's the Navy story you tell when someone asks what the service taught you? Tell it the way you'd tell it at dinner.
4. What do you know is true about running systems that most small business owners don't believe yet?
5. What made you angry enough about how small service businesses get treated (by software vendors, by agencies, by consultants) that you built a company about it?

## Section 2 — Conviction lines and enemies
*Feeds: category-of-one positioning, hooks, the "what we're against" bank.*

6. Finish these sentences fast, without thinking:
   - "Most automation consultants get it wrong because..."
   - "The biggest lie small business owners have been told about AI is..."
   - "If a plumber with $1M revenue asked me for one piece of advice, I'd say..."
   - "You should NOT buy from me if..."
7. "Automate Consistency, Not Decisions" — say the 90-second version of that idea the way you'd explain it across a truck hood, not on a slide.
8. What trend in your industry do you think is a mistake — something everyone else is selling that you refuse to?
9. Who is the enemy in your story? (Not a person — the force. E.g., "software that gets sold and never set up.")

## Section 3 — How you actually talk
*Feeds: voice & use calibration — rules and guardrails.*

10. What words and phrases do you use constantly? (Ask people around you if unsure.)
11. What words make you cringe when marketers use them? (Baseline already exists: the North Star banned-words list. Add your personal ones.)
12. Kansas City, the Navy, enterprise IT, MBA — which parts of your background do you *want* audible in the writing, and which should stay in the résumé?
13. How do you handle humor? Dry, self-deprecating, none? Give an example of a joke you'd actually make.
14. Read one of your recent LinkedIn posts out loud. Which sentences sound like you and which sound like "a marketer wrote this"? Say why.
15. When you explain something technical to a non-technical owner, what's your method? (Analogy? Story? Numbers?) Give a live example: explain a missed-call recovery workflow right now, out loud.

## Section 4 — Credibility and embodiment
*Feeds: authority anchors, embodiment content bank. Everything here must pass the verified-claims gate.*

16. What have you actually built and shipped — for yourself or anyone — that you can describe start-to-finish? (The audit tool, the calculator, internal automations. Specifics: what it does, what it replaced.)
17. Where do you use your own medicine? What's automated in *your* business today that used to eat your time?
18. What's a mistake you made in building Apex that you'd tell on stage? (Embodiment includes scar tissue.)
19. What can you legitimately claim today, with zero paying-client results? Practice saying the honest version out loud: what you've built, what you've measured on your own operation, what the enterprise career proves. (This is the Apex equivalent of "invest before the results" — the identity claim that doesn't need a fabricated case study.)

## Section 5 — The buyer relationship
*Feeds: tone-by-context table, sales-conversation calibration.*

20. Describe your favorite kind of conversation with a service business owner. What are they saying? What are you saying?
21. What do you *never* want a prospect to feel when reading your stuff? (Talked down to? Sold to? Behind the times?)
22. When an owner says "I've been burned by software before," what do you say — word for word, the way you said it last time?
23. What's your line when someone wants the cheapest option? Say the actual sentence you'd use to walk away.

## Section 6 — Boundaries and guardrails
*Feeds: the guardrails block of voice calibration.*

24. List the claims, tones, and tactics that are off-limits no matter how well they'd convert. (Known: the five projection figures are banned from copy until a real client result restores them. What else?)
25. If an AI wrote a post in your name and it went viral but didn't sound like you, would that be a win or a loss? Where exactly is the line?

---

## Output spec

Processing these answers produces `voice-calibration.md` with:

1. **Voice rules** (5–8, each with a do/don't example pulled from the answers)
2. **Guardrails** (banned claims, banned tones, the verified-claims gate)
3. **Phrase bank** (exact recurring phrases, analogies, and stories, tagged by use: hook / explanation / objection / close)
4. **Tone-by-context table** (update of the North Star table with interview evidence)
5. **Authority anchors** (verified only) and **embodiment bank** (what he's built + scar-tissue stories)
