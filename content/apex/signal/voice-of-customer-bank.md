## Pillar 1 — Symptoms

### Missed calls / dropped follow-up
- **Surface** — *"By the time we call them back, they've already booked someone else."* — North Star, validated
- **Emotional reality** — Every missed ring while he's on a job is money burning; checking voicemail at 7pm feels like opening bills. — HYPOTHESIS
- **Unspoken truth** — "I started this company so I'd stop answering to other people. Now a $200 phone runs my life." — HYPOTHESIS
- **In-the-moment** — On his back in a crawlspace, phone buzzing in his chest pocket, deciding whether to slide out for it — knowing that if he doesn't, the caller's next move is the next name on Google. — HYPOTHESIS

### Consistency requires his presence
- **Surface** — *"If I'm not watching it, it doesn't happen."* — North Star, validated
- **Emotional reality** — Vacations are half-vacations; the beach chair comes with a phone in hand. — HYPOTHESIS
- **Unspoken truth** — "If the business only works when I'm standing in it, I don't own a company — I own a job. And it might be worth nothing without me." — HYPOTHESIS
- **In-the-moment** — First morning of a family trip, at the rental-house kitchen counter at 6:40am, reading the schedule app while everyone sleeps. — HYPOTHESIS

### Marketing spend is a black box
- **Surface** — *"I'm spending money on marketing, but I don't know what's actually working."* — North Star, validated
- **Emotional reality** — Signing the agency check feels like paying a bill for something he can't see. — HYPOTHESIS
- **Unspoken truth** — "I'd never let a parts supplier bill me without an invoice I can read. I let my marketing guy do it every month because I'm embarrassed to admit I don't understand the report." — HYPOTHESIS

### Staff eats the week
- **Surface** — *"I feel like I'm constantly babysitting adults."* — North Star, validated
- **Emotional reality** — Every no-show morning starts with him re-juggling the board instead of running the business. — HYPOTHESIS
- **Unspoken truth** — "Maybe I can't keep good people because of me. I know the trade — nobody taught me to run a company." — HYPOTHESIS

### Growth without progress
- **Surface** — *"I thought growth would feel better than this."* / *"Busy is not the same as profitable."* — North Star, validated
- **Emotional reality** — Revenue is up, but so is everything else; the number gets bigger and the breathing room doesn't. — HYPOTHESIS
- **Unspoken truth** — "I'm scared I traded my thirties for a treadmill and my family got the leftovers." — HYPOTHESIS

## Pillar 2 — Desires

- **Surface** — *"I just want to know every lead is being followed up with."* — North Star, validated
- **Surface** — *"I want to step away without everything breaking."* — North Star, validated
- **Surface** — *"I want to know which marketing is actually making the phone ring."* — North Star, validated
- **Surface** — *"I don't need perfect employees, I just need things done the same way every time."* — North Star, validated
- **Emotional reality** — Wake up Monday to appointments already on the calendar that nobody in the shop touched. — North Star (paraphrase), validated
- **Unspoken truth** — "I want to be the guy at the supply house other owners ask 'how'd you do it?' — not the guy asking." — HYPOTHESIS
- **Unspoken truth** — "I want my kid to see a business worth taking over — or worth selling — not a pile of my own hours." — HYPOTHESIS

## Pillar 3 — Identity (who they're becoming)

- The owner whose company answers in 30 seconds every time — the most professional outfit in town at any size. — HYPOTHESIS
- The owner who runs the company from the truck window, not from under the house. — HYPOTHESIS
- The operator whose business is an asset: sellable, inheritable, bankable. — HYPOTHESIS
- The early adopter in a late-adopter trade — quietly two years ahead of every competitor on his street. — HYPOTHESIS

## Pillar 4 — Premium lens (what makes an offer feel like relief, not work)

- Done-for-you, not done-with-you: *"You don't set it up. We do."* — North Star, validated
- No new software for the team to learn; it wraps around how the shop already runs. — North Star (objection #1/#2 responses), validated
- The math has to answer itself: average job value × missed leads per week. — North Star, validated
- Plain-English Day-1 description — if he can't picture the first 30 days, he won't buy (the "hidden objection"). — North Star, validated
- Nobody's customer feels ignored and nobody's customer talks to a robot pretending to be human: *"Automation handles the response speed. Your team handles the relationship."* — North Star, validated

## Cost of inaction (per signal-document section 4.5)

- Every unanswered call is a job priced at his own average ticket, handed to the next name on Google. — frame validated via North Star niche statement
- The longer follow-up stays manual, the more the business's value stays trapped in his personal availability. — HYPOTHESIS
